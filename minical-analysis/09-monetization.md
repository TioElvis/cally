# 09 — Monetization

## Model [STORE]
**Freemium with ads + a one-time Pro unlock + tips.** No subscription.

## In-app purchases (US prices, 2026-11) [STORE]
| Product | Type [INFERRED] | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Pro | non-consumable | 11.99 USD |
| Tip, small ("candy" size) | consumable | 0.99 USD |
| Tip, medium ("donut" size) | consumable | 3.99 USD |
| Tip, large ("pizza" size) | consumable | 9.99 USD |

Pro facts [STORE]:
- One-time purchase, with "Restore Purchases" on the Pro screen.
- **No Family Sharing.**
- Refunds go through Apple only.

## What Pro unlocks [STORE]
No ads · unlimited templates (free: 2) · all 38 themes (free: 3) · all 10 fonts (free: default) · all 21 icons (free: 2) · copy to multiple dates · week numbers.

Note: the core features (sync, widgets, alerts, search, recurrence) are **all free**. Pro is mainly cosmetic plus ad removal.

## Ads [STORE]
- Shown in the free version. Google AdMob with mediation: LINE/LY Ads, Pangle, Mintegral, Liftoff, InMobi, ironSource, DT Exchange.
- There is a FAQ on reporting unpleasant ads, which suggests full-screen or native ads exist. Exact formats and placements are unknown, to verify in screenshots.
- ATT prompt during onboarding.

## Upsell points [STORE][INFERRED]
- Pro banner at the top of Settings.
- Lock icons on themes, fonts and icons, which lead to the paywall.
- Adding a 3rd template → paywall.
- Copy to multiple dates / week numbers → paywall.
- The paywall shows social proof (download count, rating).

## Business figures (third-party estimates, unverified)
~10–15K USD/month revenue, about 62 % ads / 38 % IAP; ~6.6M installs (AppGoblin, ScreensDesign).
